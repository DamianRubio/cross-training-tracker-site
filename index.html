Cross Training Tracker

A personal results log for cross training. It answers two questions: what did I do, and am I doing better?

What it does

  • Log training sessions made of several blocks: strength, AMRAP, EMOM, For Time, Tabata, benchmarks and more.
  • Record results in rounds, reps, time, load, distance and calories, including scaling and modified movements.
  • See personal bests and how each movement progresses over time.
  • Back up your data automatically to your own Google Drive.

What it doesn't do

No timers, no workout programming, no coaching or recommendations, no social features. It tracks and measures, nothing else.

Your data

Your data stays on your phone and in your own Google Drive. There is no server, no analytics and no advertising. Read the privacy policy.

Cross Training Tracker is an independent personal project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by any fitness brand.
