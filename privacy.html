Privacy Policy

Cross Training Tracker. Effective 22 November 2026.

Cross Training Tracker ("the app") is a personal workout results log. This policy explains what data the app handles and where it goes. The short version: your data stays on your phone and in your own Google Drive, and nobody else receives it.

Data the app stores

The app stores the training data you enter: sessions, workout blocks, movements, results, scaling choices and notes. This data is stored in a database on your device.

Images

If you import a workout from a screenshot or photo, the image is read by text recognition that runs on your device. The image is not uploaded anywhere and is not kept after the workout has been read.

Google account and Google Drive

The app asks you to sign in with Google so it can back up your data to your own Google Drive. It requests these permissions:

  • Your email address and basic sign-in identity (openid, email), used only to show which account your backups go to.
  • Access to files the app creates in your Google Drive (drive.file). The app uses this only to save and restore its backup files. It cannot see or change any other files in your Drive.

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What the app does not do

  • It does not send your data to any server run by the developer. There is no such server.
  • It does not use analytics, advertising or tracking.
  • It does not sell, share or transfer your data to anyone.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app deletes the data stored on your device. Backup files in your Google Drive stay there until you delete them, which you can do at any time from Google Drive. You can also remove the app's access to your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
